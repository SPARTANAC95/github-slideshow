What is real here, and what is not

The scale, the regions, and the neuron model are taken from published work. The connectome announced on 3 September 2026 contains 166,700 fully proofread neurons and about 125 million synapses across the brain, both optic lobes and the ventral nerve cord, sorted into 11,710 cell types, with fruitless and doublesex expression annotated so the male wiring can be compared with the female FlyWire brain.

The wiring in this page is generated, not measured. It reproduces region-level statistics (optic lobes feed the central brain, descending neurons feed the nerve cord, about 38% of neurons inhibitory) and a handful of well-studied sensorimotor pathways: sugar taste to the proboscis motor neuron MN9, bitter taste suppressing it, photoreceptors through lamina, medulla and lobula to visual projection and descending neurons, antennal touch to grooming, and pheromone to the male-specific P1 neurons, pIP10 and the wing motor neurons that make courtship song. Load an edge list exported from neuPrint or FlyWire to run the same neuron model on real connections.

Neuron model

Each neuron is a leaky integrate-and-fire unit with the parameters of the whole-brain model of Shiu et al. (Nature, 2024), which predicted proboscis extension from sugar input on the FlyWire connectome with about 95% accuracy. Every synapse adds a fixed 0.275 mV, signed by the presynaptic neuron's transmitter, and decays with a 5 ms time constant.
