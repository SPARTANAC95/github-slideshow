## 🏙️ Pebbleton: a living AI city

This repository also hosts **Pebbleton**, a browser-based social simulation of a small city
full of autonomous citizens. Every citizen has a personality, beliefs, habits and vices,
friendships, grudges, goals and memories. Nothing is scripted: groups form from friendships,
leaders emerge, rumours spread, the mayor makes unpopular decisions, protests turn into
counter-protests, and every now and then a small (non-graphic, faintly ridiculous) civil
conflict breaks out and, eventually, ends in a truce, a surrender, or a collective shrug.

- Open `city/index.html` locally, or visit `https://spartanac95.github.io/github-slideshow/city/` once GitHub Pages is enabled.
- Tap any citizen (or use the People tab) to see their mood, beliefs, relationships, group,
  memories, goals, and what they currently think of every other group, with reasons.
- The Feed shows what is happening; filter by Notable / Drama / Headlines, or follow one citizen.
- Progress autosaves in your browser. Add `#seed=anything` to the URL for a reproducible city.
- No build step and no dependencies: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
