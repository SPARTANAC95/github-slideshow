title: "A complete male fly nervous system, mapped"
---

On **3 September 2026** Google Research, HHMI Janelia, the MRC LMB and the University of Cambridge published the wiring diagram of an entire male *Drosophila* central nervous system in *Cell*.

| | |
|---|---|
| neurons | **166,700** |
| synaptic connections | **~125 million** |
| cell types | **11,710** |
| extent | brain, both optic lobes, ventral nerve cord |

The largest brain map by neuron count to date, with *fruitless* and *doublesex* expression annotated so the male wiring can be compared with the female FlyWire brain.
